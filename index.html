USB Port Connection Guide
1. Click "Pull Logs (USB Serial)" while your device is unplugged to see what ports are listed, then click Cancel.
2. Plug your StickS3 into your computer with a USB-C cable (press the side power button once if asleep to wake the port).
3. Click "Pull Logs (USB Serial)" again and select the new port that is now listed (typically named "USB JTAG/serial debug unit" or "Espressif"; on macOS: usbmodem..., on Windows: USB Serial Device (COM...)).
